About the project

ASTRO VERSE

ASTRO VERSE is a free, forever-access astronomy and astrology library. It teaches you about planets, stars, exoplanets, galaxies, black holes, missions, comets, phenomena, constellations and the stories humans have told about the sky — with discovery dates, scientific facts, and a gallery of 10,000+ procedurally generated cosmic images.

It needs no API, no server, no account and no payment. Everything runs in your browser and works offline. It was created and curated by Sabeh Fekadu.
